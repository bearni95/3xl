The Catalan show themes the map's music player plays. These are the versions sung in
Catalan, not the Japanese originals — rights stay with their owners; they are here for
the same reason the MUGEN sprite archives are.

- inuyasha-*.mp3 — InuYasha's openings and endings, 22 of them.
- one-piece-*.mp3 — One Piece's openings, endings and insert songs, 49 of them.

Most are the versions broadcast on Televisió de Catalunya. The rest say in the file name
what else they are, because a dub or a cover is not the take that went out on TV3:

- `-tvv` — Televisió Valenciana's own Catalan dub, a different recording of the same song
  (the One Piece set has 15 of these). Titled "(TVV)".
- `-charm` — Charm, a fan group; three of the InuYasha endings. Titled "(Charm)".
- `-cover-fatima-ayats` — a cover of "Before dawn". Titled "(còver)".

Where the same song was captured in more than one cut the file name says which: `-tv` and
`-restaurada` for the raw rip and the restored pass of InuYasha's "Come", `-1r` and `-2n`
for the two editions of "Angelus ~Anjerasu~", `-mugiwara-version` and `-10-years-version`
for the two mixes of "We are".

Both sets came off the Wayback Machine's captures of amiboshi.cat, which is gone —
`download-amiboshi-songs.js` at the repo root is what pulled and verified them, and it
writes a manifest recording, per song, the capture it was served and its sha256. Re-run
it to fetch them again; the mp3s it stages are git-ignored, and these are the ones that
were curated in from those staging folders. Two songs are in that pull twice over —
"Change the world" and "We are" were already vendored here before it existed, and the
pull's copies were byte-identical, so they kept the names they already had.

What each of these is — its title, and the show it opens — is not recorded here: it
is authored per file into @3xl/data's public/music.json on the admin /music screen,
which lists whatever is found in this folder. The show is kept as the same TMDB id
everything else here is keyed by, which is what puts its glyph on the song in the
player. Dropping another mp3 in is all that is needed to make it offerable there.
