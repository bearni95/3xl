The Catalan (TVC) opening themes the map's music player plays, one per show in the
game. These are the versions broadcast on Televisió de Catalunya, not the Japanese
originals — rights stay with their owners; they are here for the same reason the
MUGEN sprite archives are.

- inuyasha-change-the-world.mp3 — InuYasha, "Change the world" (català, v. TVC).
- one-piece-we-are.mp3 — One Piece, "We are" (català, v. TVC).

Which show a track belongs to is the hand-maintained list in
@3xl/shared/utils/music/tracks.ts, keyed by the same TMDB show id everything else
here is; that is what puts the show's glyph on the track in the player.
