The Catalan show themes the map's music player plays. These are the versions sung in
Catalan, not the Japanese originals — rights stay with their owners; they are here for
the same reason the MUGEN sprite archives are.

- one-piece-we-are.mp3 — One Piece, "We are" (català, v. TVC).
- inuyasha-*.mp3 — InuYasha's openings and endings, 22 of them.

Most are the versions broadcast on Televisió de Catalunya. Three are by Charm, a fan
group, and are marked `-charm` in the file name and "(Charm)" in the title, because a
cover is not what went out on air. Where the same song was captured twice the file name
says which take it is: `-tv` and `-restaurada` for the raw rip and the restored pass of
"Come", `-1r` and `-2n` for the two editions of "Angelus ~Anjerasu~".

The InuYasha set came off the Wayback Machine's capture of amiboshi.cat, which is gone —
`download-inuyasha-songs.js` at the repo root is what pulled and verified them, and it
writes a manifest recording, per song, the capture it was served and its sha256. Re-run
it to fetch them again; the mp3s it stages are git-ignored, and these are the ones that
were curated in from that staging folder.

What each of these is — its title, and the show it opens — is not recorded here: it
is authored per file into @3xl/data's public/music.json on the admin /music screen,
which lists whatever is found in this folder. The show is kept as the same TMDB id
everything else here is keyed by, which is what puts its glyph on the song in the
player. Dropping another mp3 in is all that is needed to make it offerable there.
